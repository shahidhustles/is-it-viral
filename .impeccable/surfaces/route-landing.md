## Scope and visitor mode

`/` is a **Persuade** surface for a first-time Instagram-style reel creator. Its job is to make a simulation-backed assessment feel clear, useful, and appropriately bounded before the visitor signs in.

## Audience, job, and action

The visitor needs to understand that the product compares a draft reel with a saved simulated audience—not Instagram data or a claimed ranking prediction. The primary action adapts to the visitor: signed-out visitors sign in, signed-in visitors without Account DNA begin onboarding, and returning owners continue to reel analysis. Before authentication or Account DNA resolves, the action is intentionally non-navigable and states that the workspace is being checked.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

**The Signal Ledger, opened as an evidence plate.** The route begins with a specimen-like, explicitly illustrative cohort replay: a diagram that makes the product mechanism legible before it makes any persuasive claim. It then expands into a three-stage record of Account DNA, Video DNA, and deterministic cohort simulation.

## Section system

1. **Navigation / action rail** — white paper bar, compact fingerprint stamp, one compact lime state-aware action. It keeps conversion present without competing with the evidence.
2. **Opening proposition / replay plate** — an asymmetrical paper spread with a quiet lime halo behind a product-native cohort field: a faint propagation network, not reference terrain. The hard-edged specimen teaches one causal path—seed viewers, direct shares, relevant viewers—before the carousel introduces detailed state grammar.
3. **Evidence route** — cream work surface and a full-width, divided three-part sequence. Account DNA, Video DNA, and cohort simulation use the same typographic rhythm but no repeated floating-card pattern.
4. **How it works** — a white two-column reading field. A creator-control statement anchors the left; the right is a keyboard-operable walkthrough carousel with three code-native illustrative plates: the private 100-person audience simulation, the per-second visual and audio review, and the staged first-10-viewer propagation that creates a simulated verdict. Its one slide transition makes the method inspectable, not ornamental.
5. **Advisory close** — graphite field with a single ink spark. This is the only high-contrast emotional turn and holds the full no-guarantees disclosure plus the same state-aware lime action.
6. **Footer** — quiet cream provenance line and a simple sign-in exit.

## Boundaries

Preserve the established paper, ink, cream, and restrained lime system. The focal motion is TextAnimate’s one-time, reduced-motion-safe settle on the qualifying word in the hero; the word remains legible throughout. Carousel transitions explain a manually selected product view and never autoplay. Do not introduce testimonials, creator logos, numerical outcome claims, integration badges, Instagram data claims, or generic score marketing. The illustrated replay remains labelled as a sample product view and must not be mistaken for a customer’s result.
