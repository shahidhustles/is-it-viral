## Scope and visitor mode

`/onboarding` is an **Operate** surface for authenticated social-account owners establishing their saved Account DNA.

## Audience, job, and action

An owner entering a niche, intended audience, primary language, and region needs to create a stable synthetic audience cohort that represents their account strategy. The action is to review and save that record, then continue into assessment work.

## Content and constraints

The onboarding flow must communicate advisory—not guaranteed—performance guidance. It must be authenticated and should establish that later results are simulated cohort outcomes, not access to Instagram's private ranking system.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

**The Signal Ledger:** a bright paper workbench where each answer becomes a visible evidence line in an Account DNA record. The memorable moment is the compact confirmation state: a saved account profile reads like a clear working brief, with the creator still visibly in control of what it means.
