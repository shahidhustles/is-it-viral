# 05 — Make the assessment demo-ready

**What to build:** A hackathon judge can compare prepared strong and weak reels through the real upload-to-report experience and understand the product’s advisory conclusion immediately.

**Blocked by:** 03 — Analyze an uploaded reel into Video DNA; 04 — Replay the contagion graph and inspect personas.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**UI-bearing:** Yes — upload and completed analysis-report surfaces (`/analyze`, `/analyses/[id]`).

- [ ] The product includes reusable or authorised strong and weak sample reels that execute the same persisted upload, Video DNA, simulation, recommendation, and graph workflow as a normal owner upload.
- [ ] A judge can start a sample analysis without entering Instagram credentials or relying on scraping, and can compare the resulting verdict, metrics, recommendations, and replay.
- [ ] Loading, empty, invalid-upload, transcription/analysis failure, and unavailable-report states preserve the editorial visual system and explain a safe next action.
- [ ] All user-facing copy consistently frames results as simulated audience guidance, never promises reach, growth, virality, or access to Instagram’s private algorithm.
- [ ] End-to-end verification confirms that sample outcomes originate from saved Video DNA and the deterministic simulation, rather than static report fixtures.
