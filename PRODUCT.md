# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js; Vercel AI SDK v7; React Flow for selected features; Clerk for authentication; Convex for the database.

## Users

Instagram users and other social-account owners who are trying to grow their audience. They use the product to judge whether a proposed reel or post is suited to their account's audience demographics.

## Product Purpose

Is It Viral helps account owners evaluate whether a reel or post is likely to work for their target account demographic, then offers practical recommendations for improving it. The account owner retains the final decision.

## Positioning

The product is an advisory evaluation tool for social content: it offers contextual recommendations rather than promising growth, reach, or viral results.

## Operating Context

Users evaluate social-media content—especially Instagram reels and posts—against the demographics of the account they want to grow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Assess whether a reel or post aligns with an account's target demographic.
- Recommend changes that could improve the content.
- Recommendations are guidance, not guarantees; the user makes the final publishing decision.
- Open: which social platforms beyond Instagram are supported at launch.
- Open: how content and demographic information are supplied and whether any social-account integrations are needed.
- Open: what authentication, privacy, and data-retention requirements apply.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is “Is It Viral.” It must not imply or promise virality, growth, reach, or performance outcomes.

## Evidence on Hand

No real content examples, account data, testimonials, case studies, performance benchmarks, brand assets, or approved claims have been provided. Future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Keep the creator in control of the final decision.
2. Give actionable, content-specific guidance instead of performance guarantees.
3. Ground evaluation in audience-demographic fit.
4. Communicate uncertainty honestly and avoid claims of guaranteed outcomes.
